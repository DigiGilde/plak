## Plak is here

With Plak you put a static site online, with a live version and previews. You log in with SSO Rijk.

- Choose per site who may look: everyone, whoever has a secret link, everyone with SSO Rijk, your group or people you invite.
- Publish from the admin, with the `plak` CLI, from GitHub or code.overheid.nl, or with Claude Code.
- Earlier versions are kept.
