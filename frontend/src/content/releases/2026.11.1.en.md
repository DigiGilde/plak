## Change the address of a group or site

A group administrator can change the address of the group, and a site administrator the address of the site. You do that on the "Settings" tab. The old address redirects for 30 days, for whoever may view the site, and you can change back to it during that time. After that another group or site can get the address.

Publishing from a workflow or with `plak publish` only works again after a change once you update the address there. After a change to the address of a site or its group, the "Deploy" tab of the site says so for as long as the old address still redirects.

Did the address recently belong to another site in the group? Then only a group administrator can use it, with "Use anyway". The redirect to that other site then stops at once.

## Publish a folder

You can now publish a whole folder, also for a new site. Drag the folder, or everything inside it, such as the contents of Hugo's `public/`, onto the panel, the Overview or a group page. Or use "Choose a folder". Plak packs it in your browser.

## Faster sites, less storage

Plak now sends HTML, CSS, JavaScript and other text files compressed. A site appears sooner, above all on a slow connection.

Those files are also stored compressed. A version you publish from now on takes up less room in "This site uses". Earlier versions stay as they were until you publish again.

## Last active

In "Platform administration" the column "Last activity" is now "Last active". It shows the last moment someone signed in to the admin environment or used the CLI. So someone who only works with `plak publish` shows up as active too.
