## Change the address of a group or site

A group administrator can change the address of the group, and a site administrator the address of the site. You do that on the "Settings" tab. The old address redirects for 30 days, for whoever may view the site, and you can change back to it during that time. After that another group or site can get the address.

Publishing from a workflow or with `plak publish` only works again after a change once you update the address there. After a change to the address of a site, its "Deploy" tab says so for as long as the old address still redirects.

Did the address recently belong to another site in the group? Then only a group administrator can use it, with "Use anyway". The redirect to that other site then stops at once.
